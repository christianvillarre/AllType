Who We Are

All-Type Roofing & Construction handles technically demanding work for commercial properties across Greater Houston. Our capabilities span commercial roofing, waterproofing, renovations, and emergency building repairs. We bring practical solutions and disciplined execution to projects that need more than a standard repair.

What We Do

Roof Coatings & Restoration Restore compatible commercial roof systems with protective coatings. Roof Repair & Leak Detection Find the source of roof leaks and repair affected areas. Stucco & EIFS Repair Repair damaged exterior finishes on storefronts and commercial buildings. Concrete & Masonry Repair Repair parking-area concrete, curbs, bases, and masonry. Commercial Painting Refresh commercial surfaces with cleaning, preparation, and painting. Tenant Improvements & White Box Work Prepare tenant spaces for turnover and their next use. Commercial Gutters Install commercial gutters and downspouts to manage roof drainage. Emergency Board-Up Temporarily secure damaged storefronts and exposed building openings. Commercial Waterproofing Address water intrusion at windows, wall joints, and roof-to-wall transitions.
