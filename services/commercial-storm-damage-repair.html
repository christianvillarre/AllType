Storm Recovery: Assessment & Permanent Repairs

This service focuses on documenting affected areas and planning the lasting repair scope after a storm.

Damage Assessment

Review roofing, exterior walls, openings, and site elements. Identify visible damage and areas needing further evaluation.

Permanent Repair Scope

Coordinate roof repairs or replacement, envelope repairs, damaged finishes, and related building work. Plan sequencing, materials, and tenant access around the agreed scope.

Need Immediate Help?

For active leaks or exposed roofing, use emergency roof repair . For damaged storefronts or building impact, use emergency building repair .

Planning Your Commercial Storm Damage Repair Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on affected building areas, assessment needs, protection, repair materials, and coordination across trades. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
