Handrail Installation

Installation of handrails around commercial property access routes and steps. Rail materials, dimensions, mounting surfaces, anchorage, and any required fabrication are defined before work begins.

Drain Cleanout

Drain cleanout begins by identifying the affected system, location of the blockage, and available access. Plumbing drains, roof drains, and site storm drains are assessed separately so the method and included work are clear.

Scope details

Tell us which drainage system is affected. Roof drains and scuppers receive rooftop runoff; site storm drains collect water from parking lots and paved areas; plumbing drains serve sinks and other building fixtures. Each requires a different scope and access assessment.

Roof-drain maintenance may include accessible inlet and debris clearing; related roof cleaning is covered under roof maintenance. Site storm-drain cleanout is assessed around catch basins, visible debris, and the affected drainage route. Plumbing drain cleanout is evaluated with the fixture and connected piping.

Describe where water backs up, when the problem occurs, and previous work. The proposal identifies the cleanout method, accessible areas, disposal needs, and whether additional investigation or repair is required.

Handrail Repair

Assessment and repair of damaged commercial handrails, posts, connections, and anchors. The proposal identifies component replacement, adjustment, and any specific metalwork required for the existing assembly.

Lighting & Electrical Repairs

All-Type performs commercial lighting and electrical repairs. Share the affected fixture or connection, symptoms, access requirements, and operating hours so the repair scope and any shutdown coordination can be established.

Plumbing Repairs

All-Type performs plumbing repairs for commercial properties, including assessment of affected fixtures, leaks, and connected piping. Included materials, access, and service interruptions are agreed in the repair scope.

Banner Installation

Installation of supplied banners on commercial properties, with mounting surfaces, attachment details, and access reviewed before work. Banner design and fabrication are separate from installation.

Signage Removal

Removal of commercial signage with planning for access, attachments, disposal, and the condition of the mounting surface. Electrical disconnection and patching of former attachment points are identified in the agreed scope.

Signage Installation

Installation of supplied commercial signage with review of the mounting surface, attachments, access, and any included electrical connections. Sign design and fabrication are separate services from installation.

Sink Installation

Installation or replacement of commercial sinks with review of fixture requirements and existing water and drain connections. The scope identifies the supplied fixture, connection work, access, and any related repairs.

Private-Property Stop-Sign Repair

Repair of stop-sign panels, posts, attachments, and bases in commercial parking lots and private drives. This scope concerns private-property fixtures rather than public-road traffic-control work.

Storm-Drain Cleanout

Assessment and cleanout planning for commercial site storm drains and catch basins. Describe where runoff backs up and when it occurs so accessible areas, debris removal, disposal, and any further investigation can be defined.

Stormwater Vault and Wastewater Holding Tank Cleanouts

Sediment, debris, and waste can build up inside stormwater vaults and wastewater holding tanks, reducing capacity and causing drainage or service problems. We assess access and conditions, coordinate the appropriate pumping and cleanout, and identify visible damage or follow-up repairs.

Banner Installation, Signage Installation & Removal

All-Type handles installation and removal scopes for commercial banners and signs at shopping centers and other commercial properties. We review the sign or banner, mounting surface, access, and attachment details before planning work.

Installation and removal are separate from sign design and fabrication. The proposal identifies who supplies the sign, removal and disposal needs, patching of prior attachment points, and any included electrical connection work.

Handrails & Private-Property Site Fixtures

Handrail installation and repairs are scoped around the existing metal rail, posts, connections, and anchorage. We assess damaged components and identify whether adjustment, component replacement, or specific fabrication or welding is needed; any metalwork is described in the proposal.

Private-property stop-sign repairs may include the sign panel, post, attachments, or base. This service concerns commercial parking lots and private drives, not work on public-road traffic controls. For damaged foundations, see concrete post-base repair.

Commercial Electrical, Plumbing & Sink Repairs

All-Type performs lighting and electrical repairs, plumbing repairs, and sink installation for commercial properties. We define the affected fixtures, existing connections, access, and operational needs before agreeing on the work.

Lighting and electrical scopes may involve an affected fixture or connection; plumbing scopes may involve a leaking fixture, drain issue, or sink replacement. The proposal specifies included work, materials, shutdown coordination, and any required permits or inspections.

For a tenant turnover project, include these tasks in the tenant improvement or white box scope. Rooftop equipment connections are separately identified from roof curb and flashing work.

Planning Your Commercial Property Maintenance Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on number of repair areas, required trades, repeat visits, material condition, and access. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
