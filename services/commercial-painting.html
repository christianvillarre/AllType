Bollard Painting

Preparation and repainting of commercial parking-area and walkway bollards. We review deteriorated coatings and surface condition; damaged bases or anchorage are a separate repair scope.

Fascia Painting

Preparation and painting of commercial fascia and storefront trim. We review the existing finish, access height, deteriorated areas, and color requirements before repainting.

Graffiti Removal

Graffiti treatment for commercial walls, doors, and other affected surfaces. Depending on the material and existing finish, the scope may involve removal, cleaning, surface preparation, and repainting.

Scope details

Graffiti treatment depends on the affected surface and existing finish. We assess whether cleaning or removal can preserve the surface, or whether preparation and repainting are needed.

For masonry, painted walls, metal doors, and other commercial surfaces, the proposal identifies the treatment area and finishing approach. A small test area may help establish a suitable method before treating the full affected area.

Penthouse Pressure Wash & Paint

Cleaning and painting of rooftop mechanical penthouse enclosures that house or screen building equipment. Methods are matched to the surface, with protection of adjacent roofing and equipment included in planning.

Scope details

A rooftop mechanical penthouse is the enclosure that houses or screens building equipment. We scope cleaning and painting of its exterior surfaces separately from servicing the mechanical equipment inside.

Pressure washing and other cleaning methods are selected for the surface, existing coating, and surrounding roof conditions. The work plan addresses access, protection of equipment and roof materials, drying, and repaint preparation.

Pressure Washing & Painting

Commercial surface cleaning, preparation, and repainting coordinated as one scope. Cleaning pressure and methods are selected for the material and condition, with drying and coating compatibility addressed before painting.

Accessible Parking Symbols & Pavement Markings

Painting and refreshing accessible parking symbols and agreed pavement markings on commercial properties. Confirm the approved layout and preparation; painting alone does not establish full accessibility compliance.

Scope details

We paint and refresh accessible parking symbols, pavement markings, and other agreed commercial parking-area markings. Confirm the approved layout, dimensions, colors, and surface preparation before scheduling.

Painting alone does not establish full accessibility compliance. Parking layout, routes, slopes, signage, and other site conditions are separate considerations. For sign or post repairs, see private-property site fixture services.

Planning Your Commercial Painting Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on surface area, preparation, coating selection, height, color changes, and working-hour restrictions. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
