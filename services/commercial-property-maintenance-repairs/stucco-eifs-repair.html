Awning Repair

Assessment and repair planning for storefront awnings and canopies, including damaged coverings, panels, trim, and attachments. Fabric, vinyl, aluminum, or steel components are identified before the repair materials and scope are agreed.

Scope details

We assess storefront awnings and canopies for damaged coverings, metal panels, trim, frames, and attachment points. Common material types to identify during the assessment include fabric or vinyl coverings and aluminum or steel canopy components.

Send photos of the entire awning and damaged areas so we can confirm which materials and repairs are within scope. Covering replacement, frame repairs, and attachment work are specified separately; the proposed repair depends on the existing assembly.

Stucco Ceiling Repair

Repair planning for damaged stucco ceilings and soffits at covered walkways, storefront recesses, and canopy undersides. We review overhead finish condition, substrate needs, moisture sources, and finish matching.

Scope details

Covered walkways, storefront recesses, and canopy undersides can have damaged stucco ceiling or soffit finishes. We review the affected overhead finish, attachment condition, and possible moisture source before planning repairs.

The scope identifies removal limits, substrate work where needed, finish preparation, and painting. Interior drywall ceiling work belongs on our tenant improvements page.

Exterior Column Repair

Repair of stucco, EIFS, trim, and decorative enclosures around shopping-center columns. Finish repairs are distinguished from structural column damage, which requires its own evaluation and scope.

Scope details

Exterior column repairs may involve damaged stucco, EIFS, trim, or decorative enclosures around storefront and walkway columns. We define the finish repair after reviewing the damaged area and adjacent materials.

Finish repairs are distinct from repairs to a load-bearing column. Visible movement, impact damage, or concerns about the supporting structure require further evaluation before a cosmetic patch or enclosure repair proceeds.

EIFS Repair

Repair of damaged exterior insulation and finish system areas after identifying the existing assembly and moisture concerns. Compatible materials, affected layers, joints, and finish preparation are defined in the proposal.

Scope details

All-Type repairs damaged stucco and EIFS finishes, including localized patches and the preparation needed for repainting. We identify the existing assembly, affected layers, joints, and moisture concerns before choosing repair materials.

Texture and color matching are discussed as part of the scope. A broader repaint may be appropriate where a small patch will remain visible. Water-entry repairs are coordinated with building waterproofing.

Stucco Patch & Paint

Localized stucco patching, finish preparation, and painting for commercial exteriors. The scope considers damaged substrate, moisture sources, texture matching, and the area needed to blend the finish.

Planning Your Commercial Stucco & EIFS Repair Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on repair area, substrate condition, moisture damage, lift access, and texture or color matching. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
