Emergency Board-Up

Scope details

All-Type provides emergency board-up for damaged storefronts and exposed commercial building openings. Call with the property address, affected doors or windows, photos if available, and an onsite contact who can arrange access.

The response assessment identifies temporary closure needs, attachment surfaces, occupied areas, and access routes. Board-up is temporary protection; permanent glazing, door, wall, or finish repairs are defined separately after the immediate conditions are assessed.

Planning Your Emergency Commercial Building Repair Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on opening size, damage type, temporary protection, access, materials, and permanent repair requirements. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
