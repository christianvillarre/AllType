Drywall Ceiling Repair

Repair of damaged drywall ceilings in occupied commercial spaces and tenant-turnover projects. The scope includes agreed patching, joint finishing, and paint preparation after the source of recurring damage is addressed.

Sheetrock / Drywall Repair

Repair of damaged commercial drywall, also called sheetrock, including patches, joint finishing, and paint preparation. Work is coordinated around occupants and any underlying leak or recurring damage.

Scope details

All-Type repairs drywall, also commonly called sheetrock, in commercial tenant spaces. Scopes may include damaged wall or ceiling areas, patching around prior alterations, joint finishing, and preparation for paint.

We review the source of moisture or recurring damage before closing affected areas. Ceiling access, occupied-space protection, and finish matching are coordinated with the property manager.

Tile Repair

Localized commercial tile repair with review of damaged tile, grout, and the underlying surface. The scope identifies removal, preparation, replacement material, and finishing, including whether matching tile is available.

Scope details

Localized tile repair addresses damaged or loose tile in tenant spaces and common commercial areas. We review the tile, grout, underlying surface, and availability of matching material before defining the repair boundaries.

The scope identifies removal, substrate preparation, replacement tile, and finishing. If matching tile is unavailable or the problem extends beneath the surface, we discuss a broader repair area.

White Boxing & Tenant Turnover

Preparation of a commercial tenant space for an agreed landlord or next-tenant handoff. Included demolition, drywall, ceilings, finishes, fixtures, and trade work are defined in writing because white-box requirements vary by property.

Scope details

White boxing prepares a commercial space for an agreed landlord or next-tenant handoff. Depending on the written scope, work can include removal of prior tenant finishes, drywall and ceiling repairs, surface preparation, painting, tile repairs, and agreed fixture or system work.

White box does not describe one universal finish standard. We confirm what stays, what is removed, the required condition of each space, and which plumbing or electrical tasks are included before scheduling. See electrical and plumbing repairs for those service scopes.

Planning Your Commercial Tenant Improvements Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on space size, demolition, finish selection, fixture work, included trades, and handoff requirements. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
