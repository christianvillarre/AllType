Commercial Waterproofing in Houston

Water intrusion can begin at a roof, wall, joint, window, or the transition between systems. All-Type Roofing & Construction evaluates the affected area and develops practical waterproofing and repair scopes for commercial buildings.

We focus on the building envelope as a connected system, so proposed work addresses the likely entry point and the conditions around it.

Window & Roof-Transition Waterproofing

Window & Roof-Transition Waterproofing

Assessment and repair of window-perimeter seals, storefront joints, wall penetrations, and roof-to-wall transitions. Full-roof coating work is addressed on the roof coatings and restoration page.

Scope details

We assess window-perimeter seals, storefront frame-to-wall joints, wall penetrations, and roof-to-wall transitions where water may enter a commercial building. The repair plan follows the affected assembly and connected details, not only the location of the interior stain.

Roof-edge flashing, coping, and adjoining wall conditions are reviewed together where appropriate. For coating a roof surface, use commercial roof coatings and restoration; for a roof leak requiring diagnosis, use commercial roof repair.

Trace Water Intrusion Across the Building Envelope

Water can enter at several connected details. Review the affected interior area together with the exterior surfaces and transitions above it.

Window-Perimeter Sealing

Review perimeter sealant, frame-to-wall joints, and adjacent finishes where storefront or office windows meet the wall.

Wall Joints & Exterior Transitions

Assess open or deteriorated joints, changes in exterior materials, and penetrations. Define preparation and compatible sealing details for the affected areas.

Roof-to-Wall Connections

Review parapets, counterflashing, coping, and roof-to-wall transitions. Route roof-surface coating work to commercial roof coatings and restoration.

Planning Your Commercial Waterproofing Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on investigation, joint length, removal and preparation, access height, and concealed deterioration. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
