AC Curb Installation

Installation and repair of rooftop AC curbs, surrounding flashings, and roof tie-ins. Equipment setting, ductwork, refrigerant, and electrical connections are identified separately in the agreed scope.

Scope details

All-Type installs and repairs roof curbs and the surrounding roofing details for rooftop air-conditioning equipment. We review the proposed unit location, curb dimensions, existing roof system, and access before defining the installation scope.

The roofing scope covers the curb-to-roof connection, flashing, and affected roof surfaces. HVAC equipment setting, duct connections, refrigerant work, controls, and electrical connections are separately identified and assigned in the proposal. Any required support or design evaluation is established before roof openings are made.

Roof Penetration Installation

Installation of roofing details around new pipe, duct, and equipment penetrations. Opening locations, boots, flashings, adjacent drainage, and coordination with the equipment trades are reviewed before work.

Scope details

New pipe, duct, and equipment penetrations need a planned connection to the roof system. We review opening locations, flashing or boot details, adjacent drainage, and existing roof materials before installation.

Share equipment information and the other trades’ schedules so openings, roofing tie-ins, and weather protection can be coordinated. For an existing penetration that leaks, start with commercial roof repair and leak detection.

Roof Hatch Replacement

Replacement of commercial roof hatches with review of the opening, curb, assembly, and surrounding flashing. Roof tie-ins are coordinated with building access; ladder or structural changes are separately assessed.

Scope details

Roof hatch replacement includes reviewing the existing opening, curb, hatch condition, and surrounding flashings. The agreed scope identifies the hatch assembly, curb adaptation or repair, and roof tie-in work needed for the replacement.

We plan the work around rooftop access and building operations. Ladder or access-system changes and any structural modifications are assessed separately from the hatch and roofing scope.

Skylight Installation

Installation or replacement planning for commercial skylights, including assembly, curb, flashing, and roof compatibility. New openings and powered controls may require separately defined support or electrical work.

Scope details

We assess skylight locations, existing openings, curb conditions, and roof compatibility before planning installation or replacement. The scope addresses the skylight assembly, flashing, and adjacent roof materials.

New openings may require additional design and support evaluation. Powered skylight controls or electrical connections are separate from the roofing installation and are included only when specified.

Properties & Operating Hours

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on opening size, curb condition, flashing requirements, access, equipment coordination, and support evaluation. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
