Roof Cleaning

Roof Cleaning

System-appropriate roof cleaning and debris removal for commercial properties. We assess the membrane or coating before selecting methods and identify accessible drain clearing, disposal, and repair needs separately.

Scope details

Roof cleaning is scoped to the existing roof system and its condition. We select preparation, cleaning methods, and equipment appropriate to the membrane or coating rather than assuming pressure washing is suitable for every roof.

Discuss leaves, loose debris, surface buildup, and accessible drainage points during the maintenance review. Cleaning, debris disposal, drain clearing, and any repair work are identified separately in the agreed scope.

For the distinction between roof-drain clearing, site storm drains, and plumbing drains, see drain cleanout services. Full-roof coating preparation belongs with roof coatings and restoration.

Plan Roof Care Around Your Property

Agree on maintenance tasks and visit frequency after reviewing the roof system, tree coverage, drainage, and building use.

Cleaning, Debris & Drainage

Discuss roof-compatible cleaning, debris removal, and clearing accessible roof drains, scuppers, and gutters as part of the agreed maintenance scope. Note standing water and recurring drainage problems.

Maintenance Frequency Options

Ask about quarterly, twice-yearly, or annual visits, plus inspections after significant storms. Confirm the appropriate schedule and included work in your proposal.

Condition Reporting

Request a reporting format that records roof areas, dated photos, visible findings, recommended actions, and priority. Confirm the documentation included with your inspection.

Planning Your Commercial Roof Maintenance & Inspections Service

Properties & Operating Hours

We work with Houston shopping centers, storefronts, offices, warehouses, and other commercial buildings. Share tenant hours, delivery schedules, and areas that must remain accessible so the proposed work can be coordinated around operations.

What Affects the Price?

Pricing depends on roof size, cleaning scope, drainage access, visit frequency, and reporting requirements. Assessment establishes the included work, exclusions, and any further evaluation needed before a project price is agreed.

What to Send Us

Provide the property address, onsite contact, affected locations, photos, relevant drawings or past repair information, and desired timing. Identify active damage, tenant concerns, and roof or equipment access restrictions.

Assessment Through Completion

We review the conditions, define the proposed scope, and agree on scheduling and access. At completion, review the agreed work and any remaining items with the project contact. Confirm documentation and follow-up requirements in the proposal.
