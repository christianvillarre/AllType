Know the Signs

When Repair
Isn't Enough

Repeated repairs are a warning sign, not a solution. If your roof is showing any of these conditions, a full replacement is likely the more cost-effective path — and we'll give you an honest assessment either way.

01

Recurring Leaks

If you're patching the same areas season after season, the underlying system has failed. Continued repairs only delay the inevitable and add cost over time.

02

Roof Age

Most commercial roofing systems have a lifespan of 15–25 years depending on type and maintenance. An aging roof nearing end-of-life is a liability.

03

Widespread Membrane Failure

Extensive cracking, bubbling, or separation across the membrane surface means the system has broken down and can no longer be spot-repaired effectively.

04

Saturated Insulation

Water-logged insulation beneath the membrane dramatically reduces energy efficiency and can lead to structural deck damage if left unaddressed.

05

Storm or Catastrophic Damage

Severe hail, high winds, or a major storm event can compromise a roof beyond what patchwork repairs can address — especially on older systems.

06

Energy Cost Increases

A failing roof with compromised insulation drives up HVAC costs. A new roofing system with modern materials can significantly reduce energy expenses.

Roofing Systems

Systems We
Install

01

TPO Roofing Systems

A popular single-ply membrane known for heat-reflective properties and strong weld seams. Excellent for Houston's UV-intense climate.

02

EPDM Rubber Roofing

A highly durable synthetic rubber membrane with a long proven track record on low-slope commercial roofs and industrial buildings.

03

PVC Roofing Systems

A chemical-resistant single-ply option ideal for buildings with rooftop equipment, grease exposure, or demanding performance requirements.

04

Metal Roofing Systems

Standing seam and exposed fastener metal roofing built for long-term durability — ideal for warehouses, manufacturing, and large-span structures.

05

Modified Bitumen

A tough, multi-layer asphalt-based system with excellent resistance to punctures, tears, and extreme temperature cycling.

06

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

A time-tested multi-ply roofing system that provides exceptional waterproofing and redundancy for demanding commercial applications.

07

Recover & Re-Roofing

When the existing deck is sound, a re-cover installation over the current system can reduce cost and project timeline significantly.

08

Flat & Low-Slope Systems

Specialized solutions for flat roof geometry — addressing drainage, ponding water, and performance for the most common commercial roof type.

Each roofing system is selected based on your building type, budget, and long-term performance goals. We'll help you make the right call.

Our Process

How It
Works

A roof replacement is a significant investment. We keep the process transparent, efficient, and as minimally disruptive to your operations as possible — from first call to final inspection.

Step 01

Roof Assessment

We inspect the existing system, deck condition, insulation, and drainage to determine what needs to be replaced and what can be preserved.

Step 02

System Selection

We walk you through your options — membrane type, insulation R-value, and installation method — matched to your building and budget.

Step 03

Tear-Off & Prep

Existing roofing material is removed, the deck is inspected and repaired as needed, and the surface is prepped for the new system.

Step 04

New System Install

Your selected roofing system is installed by our trained crews following manufacturer specifications — ensuring warranty validity and long-term performance.

Step 05

Final Inspection

We conduct a full inspection before project closeout and provide documentation including photos, warranty information, and a final report.
