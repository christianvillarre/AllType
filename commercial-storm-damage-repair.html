Recovery process

Assess, protect, repair

Every storm leaves a different pattern of damage. The work is scoped to the property, the affected materials, and the needs of the people using the building.

  1. Describe the storm damageTell us the location, building type, and the areas you know are affected.
  2. Assess the propertyWe review visible damage and identify where additional construction evaluation is needed.
  3. Define immediate protectionWe discuss how to limit further exposure while the permanent repair scope is developed.
  4. Plan and perform repairsWe coordinate the agreed construction work with access, operations, and long-term building performance in mind.
