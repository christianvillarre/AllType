Situations That
Need Immediate
Attention

Every hour a roof failure goes unaddressed, the damage gets worse and the cost gets higher. If you're dealing with any of the following, call us now — not Monday morning.

01

Active Interior Leaks

Water entering your building puts inventory, equipment, electrical systems, and structural materials at risk. Every minute counts when a leak is active.

02

Storm & Hurricane Damage

High winds, hail, and severe weather can tear membranes, blow off flashing, and leave your roof completely exposed. Post-storm response needs to happen fast.

03

Wind Blow-Offs & Uplift

When wind pulls back or removes sections of roofing material, the exposed deck must be secured immediately to prevent further structural damage and water infiltration.

04

Roof Collapse Risk

Structural damage, excessive ponding water, or debris loading can create a collapse risk. If you suspect your roof is under dangerous stress, evacuate and call immediately.

05

Sudden Membrane Failure

A membrane that tears, separates, or splits open during a weather event leaves no protection. Rapid deployment of temporary cover stops the bleeding while a repair is planned.

06

Flooding After Heavy Rain

Houston's intense rainfall events can overwhelm compromised drainage systems and pooling water can find any weakness in your roof. Fast action limits interior damage.
