What We Do

Painting
Services

We handle the full scope of commercial painting — from prep and priming through final coat. Our crews are trained for commercial and industrial environments and equipped for large-scale projects.

01

Exterior Building Painting

Full repaint or touch-up of commercial exteriors. We prep surfaces properly — cleaning, scraping, caulking, and priming — before applying coatings built for Houston's heat and humidity.

02

Interior Commercial Painting

Warehouses, office spaces, retail floors, and common areas. Low-VOC options available. We coordinate with your operations to minimize disruption and downtime.

03

Industrial Coatings

Heavy-duty protective coatings for manufacturing plants, refineries, and industrial facilities. Includes rust inhibitors, chemical-resistant coatings, and specialty systems.

04

Epoxy Floor Systems

High-performance epoxy coatings for warehouse floors, loading docks, and production areas. Durable, easy to clean, and available in a range of finishes and colors.

05

Metal Building Painting

Specialized coatings for metal panel buildings, steel structures, and pre-engineered metal systems. Proper etching, priming, and topcoat selection for lasting adhesion.

06

Parking Lot & Safety Marking

Line striping, safety markings, directional arrows, ADA compliance markings, and fire lane designations for parking areas and warehouse floors.
