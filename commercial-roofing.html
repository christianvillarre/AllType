Flat Roofing Systems

Commercial flat roofing for Houston buildings.

Flat and low-slope roofing is the standard for commercial buildings, shopping centers, and warehouses. We install and service all major flat roofing membrane systems designed to handle Houston's intense UV exposure, heat, and heavy rainfall.

Most Popular

TPO Roofing
(Thermoplastic Polyolefin)

The leading flat roofing membrane for commercial buildings. TPO reflects UV rays, reduces cooling costs, and delivers excellent puncture resistance — ideal for Houston's climate. Heat-welded seams create a watertight, seamless surface.

Lifespan20–30 years
Best forOffices, retail, warehouses
Energy savingsHigh (white reflective)
WarrantyUp to 20-year manufacturer
Proven & Reliable

EPDM Roofing
(Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)

A synthetic rubber membrane with a 50-year track record in commercial roofing. EPDM is highly flexible, handles extreme temperature swings, and resists ozone and UV degradation. Available in black or white membranes.

Lifespan25–35 years
Best forLarge flat commercial roofs
MaintenanceVery low
WarrantyUp to 25-year manufacturer
Heavy-Duty

Modified Bitumen
(Mod-Bit)

A multi-layer asphalt-based system reinforced with fiberglass or polyester. Mod-Bit performs exceptionally well on roofs with foot traffic or mechanical equipment and delivers outstanding protection against ponding water.

Lifespan20–25 years
Best forHigh-traffic commercial roofs
DurabilityExcellent
RepairabilityVery easy

Metal Roofing Systems

Metal roofing built to last decades.

Metal roofing has become increasingly popular for commercial buildings in Houston due to its long lifespan, low maintenance requirements, and resistance to wind damage — a critical consideration in a hurricane-prone region. We install standing seam, corrugated, and metal panel systems for commercial and industrial applications.

Premium

Standing Seam Metal

The highest-performing metal roofing system. Concealed fasteners eliminate exposed screws that can loosen over time. Panels interlock at raised seams, creating a clean, watertight surface that allows for thermal expansion without leaking.

Lifespan40–60 years
Wind resistanceUp to 140 mph
Best forCommercial, industrial, retail
MaintenanceMinimal
Cost-Effective

Corrugated Metal Panels

A cost-effective, durable solution widely used on agricultural, warehouse, and industrial buildings. Corrugated metal is quick to install, lightweight, and available in galvanized steel, Galvalume, and aluminum for corrosion resistance.

Lifespan25–40 years
Best forWarehouses, agriculture, industrial
CostLower upfront
Install speedFast
Re-Roofing Option

Metal Retrofit / Recover

Install a new metal roof directly over an existing flat or low-slope roof without a full tear-off. Metal retrofit systems eliminate costly disposal, reduce installation time, and add a weather-tight second layer of protection to aging buildings.

Tear-off requiredNo
Cost savings20–30% vs. full tear-off
Lifespan added30–50 years
DisruptionLow

FAQ

Common questions about commercial roofing.

In Houston's climate, a well-installed TPO or EPDM flat roof typically lasts 20–30 years with regular maintenance. The intense UV exposure, heat, and heavy rainfall accelerate wear faster than in cooler climates, which is why proper installation and annual inspections are critical. Modified bitumen systems last 20–25 years, while a metal roof can last 40–60 years.

For most Houston shopping centers and strip malls, TPO membrane roofing is the most popular and practical choice. It's highly reflective (reducing cooling loads), durable against UV exposure, cost-effective for large roof areas, and relatively easy to repair. For owners looking for a long-term investment with minimal ongoing maintenance, a standing seam metal roof or metal retrofit system is also worth considering.

Yes — in many cases, a metal retrofit or recover system can be installed directly over an existing flat roof without a full tear-off. This eliminates costly demolition and disposal, reduces installation time, and saves 20–30% compared to a full replacement. We'll inspect your existing roof to determine if it's a good candidate for a retrofit system.

It depends on the age and condition of the existing system. If your roof is less than 15 years old, has isolated damage, and the membrane is otherwise sound, repairs are often the right call. If there's widespread membrane failure, recurring leaks in multiple areas, or the system is near the end of its rated lifespan, replacement is usually more cost-effective in the long run. We'll give you an honest assessment either way.

Yes. We have extensive experience working alongside insurance adjusters and can provide the detailed damage documentation needed to support your claim. We'll inspect the roof, document all damage thoroughly, and work with your insurer to make sure the scope of work is accurately captured. We do not charge for storm damage inspections.

Commercial roofing costs vary significantly based on roof size, slope, system type, condition of the existing roof, and access requirements. As a general range: TPO and EPDM systems typically run $5–$12 per square foot installed; modified bitumen runs $6–$10 per sq ft; standing seam metal runs $12–$20+ per sq ft. Large-scale projects often benefit from lower per-square-foot pricing. We provide free written estimates so you know exactly what you're getting.
