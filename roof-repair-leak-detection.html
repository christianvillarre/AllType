Our Approach

How We
Find It

Roof leaks rarely appear where you think they do. Our step-by-step diagnostic process locates the true source — not just the symptom — before any repair work begins.

Step 01

On-Site Inspection

We perform a full visual inspection of your roof surface, membrane seams, flashing, penetrations, drains, and parapet walls to identify all potential problem areas.

Step 02

Infrared & Moisture Scanning

For complex or hidden leaks, we use infrared thermal imaging to detect wet insulation and trapped moisture beneath the membrane surface — without tearing anything up.

Step 03

Diagnostic Report

You receive a clear written report outlining the leak source, affected areas, and our recommended repair plan — with honest options and no upsells.

Step 04

Repair & Verification

Once approved, we complete repairs using materials matched to your existing system. We verify the fix before we leave — and follow up to make sure the problem is solved.

Common Causes

Why Commercial
Roofs Leak

Houston's climate — heavy rain, high UV exposure, and dramatic temperature swings — puts more stress on commercial roofs than most regions in the country.

Most leaks aren't random. They develop at predictable failure points over time. Knowing where to look is half the battle — and our team knows exactly where to start.

01Failed or separated membrane seams
02Deteriorated flashing at walls, curbs & edges
03Clogged or inadequate roof drainage
04Punctures from foot traffic or equipment
05UV degradation and membrane shrinkage
06Storm, hail, or wind uplift damage
07Failed pipe boots & penetration seals
