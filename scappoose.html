Who we are

A hospitality group rooted in the Pacific Northwest

Founded in Scappoose, Oregon — a small city nestled between the Coast Range and the Columbia River — we've grown from a single property into one of the region's most respected hospitality operators.

We believe that great hotels don't just serve guests — they anchor communities, create meaningful employment, and reflect the spirit of the places they call home. Every property we operate carries that philosophy forward.

From select-service hotels near Portland's airport corridor to full-service properties in coastal and mountain gateway communities, we bring the same standard of care to every guest experience.

What we do

Full-spectrum hospitality expertise

Hotel development
We identify, underwrite, and develop new hotel projects in Pacific Northwest markets — from site selection and brand negotiation through design, permitting, and construction management.
Ownership & investment
We acquire and hold hotel assets with a long-term ownership philosophy, partnering with institutional and private investors who share our commitment to sustainable value creation.
Hotel management
Our operations team manages branded and independent properties with a hands-on approach — delivering top-quartile RevPAR, guest satisfaction scores, and employee retention across our portfolio.
