All-Type Roofing and All-Type Construction are Houston-based teams with decades of experience delivering commercial roofing and construction solutions across Texas and the Southeast. What Drives Us

For over two decades, All-Type Roofing and Construction have built long-term relationships by delivering consistent, high-quality commercial roofing and construction work across Houston and Texas. Our team has been recognized by the Better Business Bureau with a Gold Star Certificate for outstanding customer relations.

We work directly with property owners, engineers, and facility managers to solve real-world roofing and construction challenges. From small repairs to large-scale commercial projects, we focus on practical solutions, clear communication, and results that last.

As a Houston-based company, we take pride in improving commercial buildings throughout the city and surrounding areas. Our leadership is rooted here, and we understand the demands of maintaining and protecting commercial properties in this region.

Every commercial roofing and construction project begins with a free estimate and a clearly defined scope of work. Our team stays responsive, on schedule, and aligned with each client's specific needs from start to finish.

Safety is a priority on every job site. We strictly follow all OSHA guidelines to ensure projects are completed safely, efficiently, and without compromise.
