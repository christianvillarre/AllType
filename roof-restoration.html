Coating Systems

Systems We Apply

We match the coating system to your existing roof type, climate exposure, and performance requirements. Each system has distinct strengths — we'll help you choose the right one.

01

Silicone Roof Coatings

The most popular restoration coating for Houston's climate. Silicone holds up exceptionally well against standing water and UV degradation — it won't harden, crack, or chalk over time.

Best For TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, and metal roofs with ponding water concerns
02

Acrylic Roof Coatings

A water-based, highly reflective coating that excels in high-UV environments. Excellent for energy savings and available in white or light colors to maximize solar reflectance.

Best For Metal roofs, BUR systems, and low-slope roofs in dry or moderate climates
03

Polyurethane Coatings

A two-part coating offering outstanding impact and abrasion resistance. Ideal for roofs with heavy foot traffic or mechanical equipment that creates wear over time.

Best For Roofs with high foot traffic, rooftop equipment, or puncture-prone surfaces
04

Butyl Rubber Coatings

A flexible, waterproof coating that performs well on roofs experiencing significant thermal movement and expansion — particularly around seams, penetrations, and edges.

Best For Metal roofs, aged EPDM systems, and buildings with notable thermal cycling
05

Spray Polyurethane Foam (SPF)

A seamless, self-flashing insulation and waterproofing system sprayed directly onto the roof surface. Eliminates seams entirely and adds meaningful R-value to the building envelope.

Best For Irregular roof geometry, flat roofs needing insulation upgrades, and energy-focused projects
06

Reflective & Cool Roof Systems

Energy-performance coatings designed to meet ENERGY STAR and Cool Roof Rating Council standards — reducing surface temperatures by up to 50°F and cutting cooling loads significantly.

Best For Any commercial roof where energy savings and reduced HVAC costs are a priority

Our Process

How Restoration
Works

Restoration done right isn't just painting a roof. Every project follows a disciplined process — repairing deficiencies first so the coating performs as intended for its full warranted life.

Step 01

Roof Assessment

We inspect the existing membrane, seams, flashing, insulation, and deck to determine whether restoration is viable and which coating system is the right fit.

Step 02

Surface Preparation

The entire roof surface is cleaned — pressure washed and treated as needed — to remove dirt, debris, algae, and any contaminants that would compromise coating adhesion.

Step 03

Repair & Reinforcement

All seams, flashing, penetrations, cracks, and blisters are repaired and reinforced before coating begins. A coating applied over unaddressed defects will fail prematurely.

Step 04

Coating Application

The selected coating is applied at the specified mil thickness — in one or two coats depending on the system — ensuring full coverage and consistent film build across the entire surface.

Step 05

Inspection & Documentation

We inspect the finished coating for coverage and quality before project closeout. You receive a full report with photos and warranty documentation for your records.
