All-Type Roofing and All-Type Construction are Houston-based teams delivering commercial roofing and construction solutions across Texas and the Southeast.

With over 100 years of combined industry experience, we bring proven expertise, fast response, and reliable execution to every project.

For over two decades, we have built long-term relationships through consistent, high-quality work and responsive service. Our team has been recognized by the Better Business Bureau with a Gold Star Certificate for outstanding customer relations.

We work directly with property owners, engineers, and managers to solve real-world roofing and construction challenges. From small repairs to large-scale projects, our focus is practical solutions, clear communication, and results that last.
